Zhehao Li 李喆昊

       

I am a second-year CS Ph.D. student at Stanford University, advised by Prof. Doug James. Currently, my research interest lies in animation control and physics-based multimodal (visual-audio) simulation.

I received my M.S. from Graphics & Geometric Computing Lab at University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) , where I was very fortunate to be advised by Prof. Ligang Liu. I also worked with Prof. Bo Ren at Nankai University and Prof. Tao Du at the Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS), Tsinghua University. I received my B.E. in CS from USTC.
