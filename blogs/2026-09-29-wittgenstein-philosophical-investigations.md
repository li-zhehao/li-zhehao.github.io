Notes collected while reading Ludwig Wittgenstein's *Philosophical Investigations*, and a commentary on it.

## On writing

From Wittgenstein's own preface:

> I should not like my writing to spare other people the trouble of thinking. But, if possible, to stimulate someone to thoughts of his own.

## On formulating the problem

From Michael O'Sullivan's [*An Analysis of Ludwig Wittgenstein's Philosophical Investigations*](https://books.google.com/books?id=eEkrDwAAQBAJ) (Macat Library, CRC Press, 2017), on how Wittgenstein works:

> He believes the best way to solve a problem is to formulate it correctly.

A reminder that much of the difficulty in a problem lives in how it is posed, not only in how it is solved.
